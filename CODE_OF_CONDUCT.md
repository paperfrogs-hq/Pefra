# Code of Conduct

Paperfrogs HQ wants discussions around Pefra to be constructive and welcoming. This code of conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and assume good faith.
- Give feedback on ideas and work, not on personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized comments or attention, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email Paperfrogs HQ privately at [hello@paperfrogs.dev](mailto:hello@paperfrogs.dev). Include relevant links and context. Do not post sensitive personal details in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed with discretion, and information will be shared only as needed to address the concern.
